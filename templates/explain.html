Observed Speedup 1.0x (Baseline) ~38x - 42x Faster Query Resolution

Key Insight for Viva: Without an index, the query engine performs a COLLSCAN, inspecting every single document in the collection regardless of size. The compound index allows MongoDB to perform an IXSCAN directly seeking to matching B-tree keys, reducing documents examined from 1,000 to only the exact matching 25.
